AWS announces general availability of a new Local Zone in Hanoi, Vietnam, supporting EC2 instances, S3 with One Zone-Infrequent Access, EBS, ECS, EKS, VPC, Direct Connect, and Application Load Balancer. It is one of the first AWS Local Zones in Asia Pacific with support for Amazon S3 and EBS Local Snapshots.
